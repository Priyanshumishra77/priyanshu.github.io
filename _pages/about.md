# Priyanshu Mishra

**CPU / SoC Design Verification Engineer | RISC-V | DSP/ASIP | Processor HW/SW Co-Verification**

I am a semiconductor design-verification engineer with **nearly five years of hands-on experience** across RISC-V processors, SoC infrastructure IP, custom DSP/ASIP verification, and processor architecture. My work combines SystemVerilog/UVM verification with software-driven testing, compiler-generated workloads, instruction-set simulators, and architectural result correlation.

I currently work at **ACL Digital** on a **Renesas client project**, performing HW/SW co-verification of a custom DSP/ASIP generated using Synopsys ASIP Designer. My focus includes connecting compiler and SDK outputs to RTL simulation, correlating ISS behavior with RTL execution, and investigating architectural discrepancies.

Previously, I worked on a **16 nm TrustZone-enabled AI SoC for NXP** at SiSoC Semiconductor Technologies. I owned verification activities across eDMA5, AHB system control, MMU, LLC, and PMUX, including UVM environment bring-up, verification planning, software-driven tests, regression automation, and pre-silicon RTL debug.

## Engineering focus

- **Processor verification:** RISC-V RV32/RV64, ISA and privileged architecture, CSRs, traps, exceptions, privilege modes, virtual memory, pipelines, hazards, and LSU behavior.
- **DSP/ASIP HW/SW co-verification:** Synopsys ASIP Designer/Programmer, GCC, Clang, Chess compiler, generated firmware images, ISS-to-RTL functional and architectural correlation.
- **SoC IP verification:** SystemVerilog, UVM, directed and constrained-random testing, functional coverage, assertions, vPlans, regression automation, and waveform-based root-cause analysis.
- **Memory and interconnect:** DMA/eDMA, AHB, AXI, APB, MMU, TLB, SRAM lookup structures, cache behavior, arbitration, and TrustZone access control.
- **AI and heterogeneous compute:** experience verifying infrastructure around an AI SoC integrating CPU, GPU, NPU, and DSP subsystems; continuing independent study of accelerator architecture.
